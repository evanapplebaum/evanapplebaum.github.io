Ride-On Electric Car [WIP]

After retiring my electric skateboard (and a titanium pin in my pinky), I rebuilt a non-functional kids' Lamborghini Revuelto into an adult-rideable vehicle. Extended the rear skateboard truck to fit the wheelbase using a diamond tooth saw and cobalt drill bits, reinforced the chassis with steel axles and a repurposed deck, and rewired the 12V accessory system via splicing and soldering.
